Are there specific rules for certain user agents?

Some bots are completely blocked: 
sitecheck.internetseer.com, Zealbot, MSIECrawler, SiteSnagger, WebStripper, WebCopier,

wget, grub-client

Wikipedia internal bots are allowed:
IsraBot, Orthogaffe


Websites use robots.txt to tell bots what they can and can’t access. 
It helps keep servers from getting too busy and protects private pages.
